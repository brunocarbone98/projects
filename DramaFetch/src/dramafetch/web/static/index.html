DramaFetch

Download the TikTok short-drama episodes your account can already watch, a whole series at a time, for personal offline viewing.

Access token

A session cookie (HttpOnly, 30 days) remembers it; the page itself stores nothing.

1. Series

The long number is the series id; the trailing number is only the episode you were watching. Resolving opens TikTok in a browser on this machine and usually takes 10–40 s; the list is kept for 15 minutes, so downloading right after it costs no second visit (Refresh list fetches it again). Then Download all available is one click — every free episode, and every paid one your own TikTok account has unlocked (paid · unlocked: downloaded with your session, like a free one) — and every episode it downloads gets a Save link for this device. Covers are embedded in the video by default (serve --thumbnail both saves a separate image as well); the Cover link gives you the embedded one, or the file saved next to the video.

Several series

Every free or unlocked episode of every series, one series at a time: each series is resolved right before its own downloads (never all the lists up front), with a rest between two series that contact TikTok, and the first sign of trouble — a refusal, a login wall — cancels the series still waiting, without another request. A series already complete on disk costs no request for 24 hours. Up to 50 links; blank lines and lines starting with # are skipped. When a series is done, Save all (ZIP) copies the whole series to this device in one file — stored, never recompressed, so picture and sound are untouched.
