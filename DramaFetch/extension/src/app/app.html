Your TikTok library

· ·

To update, open your TikTok profile → Favorites → Drama in a tab of this Chrome.

No password and no separate API: DramaFetch reads the same list your profile shows, from your own TikTok tab, with pauses. Nothing downloads until you ask.

downloaded one after the other, with the usual pauses

Reading the whole list takes 1–2 minutes (about 50 pages, a pause before each). “On disk” = every episode your account may watch, saved and checked. Download selected downloads the ticked dramas one series after the other, exactly as Download all would.

Remove from your Favorites on TikTok?

    DramaFetch will open the drama in a background tab, press its Bookmark button once and check, after reloading the page, that TikTok kept the change. If you change your mind, press Bookmark again on TikTok.
